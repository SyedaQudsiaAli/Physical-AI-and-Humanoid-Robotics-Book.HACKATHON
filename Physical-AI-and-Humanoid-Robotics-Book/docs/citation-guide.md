# Citation Management System for Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics Book

## Overview
This document outlines the citation management system and workflow for APA style citations in the Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics Book project.

## Citation Standards

### APA Style Requirements
- All citations must follow 7th edition APA format
- Minimum 50% of citations must be from peer-reviewed robotics/AI papers published within the last 10 years
- Official technical documentation is acceptable for the remaining citations
- All citations must be traceable to their original sources

### Citation Format Examples

#### Journal Articles
Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, volume(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xx.xxx/yyyy

Example:
Khatib, O., Park, H. J., & Bicchi, A. (2016). Robot collisions: A survey on detection, isolation, identification, and accommodation. *IEEE Transactions on Robotics*, 32(6), 1264-1276. https://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2016.2605650

#### Conference Proceedings
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year, Month). Title of paper. In *Proceedings of Conference Name* (pp. page range). Publisher. DOI or URL

Example:
Brooks, R. A. (1986, August). A robust layered control system for a mobile robot. In *Proceedings of IEEE Conference on Robotics and Automation* (pp. 854-860). IEEE. https://doi.org/10.1109/CRA.1986.1087067

#### Technical Documentation
ROS. (n.d.). ROS 2 documentation. *Robot Operating System*. https://docs.ros.org/en/humble/

#### Books
Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle*. Publisher. URL if from database

Example:
Siciliano, B., & Khatib, O. (Eds.). (2016). *Springer handbook of robotics* (2nd ed.). Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-32552-1

## Citation Management Workflow

### 1. Research and Collection
- Identify relevant peer-reviewed papers from top robotics conferences (ICRA, IROS, CoRL, RSS)
- Collect official technical documentation from ROS 2, NVIDIA Isaac, Gazebo, etc.
- Verify publication dates to ensure papers are from the last 10 years
- Record full citation information immediately to avoid loss

### 2. Organization
- Use a reference manager (Zotero, Mendeley, or similar) to organize citations
- Tag citations by topic (ROS 2, Gazebo, Isaac, LLMs, etc.)
- Maintain a master bibliography file in APA format
- Include links to DOI or official documentation when available

### 3. Integration
- Integrate citations directly into Markdown files using APA in-text citation format
- Use numbered citations in square brackets [1], [2], [3] or author-date format (Author, Year)
- Maintain a reference list at the end of each chapter
- Ensure all in-text citations have corresponding full citations in the reference list

### 4. Verification
- Verify that each citation links to a valid source
- Check that peer-reviewed papers meet the 10-year requirement
- Confirm that official documentation is current
- Ensure all citations follow proper APA 7th edition format

## Tools and Resources

### Recommended Reference Managers
- **Zotero**: Free, open-source, with excellent web browser integration
- **Mendeley**: Free, with good PDF annotation capabilities
- **EndNote**: Commercial option with advanced features

### Key Databases for Robotics Research
- IEEE Xplore Digital Library
- ACM Digital Library
- ScienceDirect
- Google Scholar
- DBLP Computer Science Bibliography

### Primary Sources to Prioritize
- International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)
- IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)
- Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL)
- Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS)
- Official documentation from ROS 2, NVIDIA Isaac, Gazebo, Unity Robotics, etc.

## Quality Assurance
- Regular review of citation quality and relevance
- Verification that minimum 50% peer-reviewed requirement is met
- Cross-checking citations for accuracy and completeness
- Ensuring citations support the claims made in the text

- [ ] All citations are traceable to original sources
- [ ] In-text citations match reference list entries
